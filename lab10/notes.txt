Lab 10 - notes (Part 3)

1. In "trace 32 grep hello README" only read is printed because 32 = 2^5, so only bit 5 is set,
   and 5 is SYS_read. The bits for open (15) and close (21) are 0, so syscall() does not print them.

2. In forkforkfork several pids print lines because of this line from Part 2.5 in fork():
   np->trace_mask = p->trace_mask;
   The child copies the mask of its parent, so every descendant also traces its own fork calls.

3. echo calls write twice: once for "hi" (2 bytes) and once for the newline (1 byte).
   The kernel prints the trace line after each call finishes, so the line "write -> 2" of the
   first call appears right after "hi" and before the newline written by the second call.

4. open (15) and close (21): 2^15 + 2^21 = 32768 + 2097152 = 2129920.
